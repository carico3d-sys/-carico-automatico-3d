1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files saved by the browser on your device. This website uses exclusively technical and security cookies, necessary for the service to function. We do not use profiling cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking tools.

2. Cookies Used

Cookie Purpose Type Duration
sessionid Django authentication session (keeps you logged in) Technical Session (until browser is closed)
csrftoken Protection against CSRF attacks on forms Technical Session
cb_demo Signed anti-abuse token: prevents obtaining more than one free trial from the same device Security / Anti-fraud 365 days
cb_fp Browser fingerprint hash for demo anti-abuse control Security / Anti-fraud 365 days

The cb_demo and cb_fp cookies are not used for profiling: they contain only pseudonymized values (hashes) used for the anti-abuse control described in the Privacy Policy (legal basis: legitimate interest).

3. Managing Cookies from the Browser

You can view, block, or delete cookies from your browser settings. Please note that blocking technical cookies may cause the service to malfunction (e.g., login or data saving).
